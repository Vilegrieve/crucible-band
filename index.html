The Onslaught

Forged in the gritty underbelly of Prince George, British Columbia, Crucible of Scorn delivers an unrelenting fusion of classic thrash speed and bone-crushing death metal heaviness.

Built on aggressive riffing, punishing percussion, and guttural storytelling, the band brings high-energy live onslaughts to stages across Western Canada.

MIKE

Vocals-Guitar

Rythmic Warcaller

Spitting gravel and guttural tales that detail survival, glory, murder and triumph.

SEAN

Lead Guitar

Stringed Aggression

Commanding the fretboard with high-speed, sharp, aggressive death thrash patterns and blistering melodic hooks.

ERIC

Bass Guitar

Bass Obliteration

Laying down the deep, rumbling low-end armor that adds physical weight and cavernous, bone-shattering force to the rhythm.

CHRIS

Drums

Percussive Onslaught

Driving the speed with endless blastbeats, caveman double-bass thuds, and chaotic thrash momentum from behind the kit.
