The Onslaught

Forged in the gritty underbelly of Prince George, British Columbia, Crucible of Scorn delivers an unrelenting fusion of classic thrash speed and bone-crushing death metal heaviness.

Built on aggressive riffing, punishing percussion, and guttural storytelling, the band brings high-energy live onslaughts to stages across Western Canada.

MIKE

Vocals-Guitar

Rythmic Warcaller

ERIC
